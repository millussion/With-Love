What is With Love?

With Love is a photo editor for the viral star scatter photo edit, the star cutout trend and polka dot cutout edits. Upload a photo or a 5-second video, choose a star, sparkle or polka dot motif, add your caption, then share an aesthetic story card or a 12-second story video to Instagram.

How to make a With Love

  1. Add a photo or a 5-second video, or take one with the camera.
  2. Choose a star, sparkle or polka dot motif, a frame and a paper.
  3. Add a place, a date or a feeling.
  4. Save or share your story card to Instagram.
